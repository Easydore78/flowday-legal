Delete Your FlowDay Account

To delete your FlowDay data:

  1. Google Drive data: Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, find FlowDay and click "Remove Access". All your data will be deleted from Google Drive.
  2. Local data: Uninstall the FlowDay app from your device.
  3. Contact: For any deletion request, contact flowday.app@outlook.com

Processing time: 30 days maximum.
