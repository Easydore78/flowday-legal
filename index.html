FlowDay

A visual planner to stay focused on what matters.

Not everything. Just now.

FlowDay helps people with attention difficulties (ADHD) visualize their day in real time and stay focused on the next step — one task at a time, without feeling overwhelmed by a full list.

Features

📱Main tile with real-time countdown and progress bar
🚶Travel time built into each activity
🔔Configurable notifications and alarms per activity
📅Multi-day planning with daily, weekly, or custom recurrence
🎨5 theme colors, light/dark/auto mode
📲Android home screen widget
